## Changes on 2025-09-19

### Frontend UI Development
- Marked "Frontend UI Geliştirmesi" as complete in `TODO.md`.

### Backend API Update
- Added `asset_type` parameter to `/api/v1/signals` and `/api/v1/market_data` endpoints in `src/optitrade/api/server.py`.
- Propagated `asset_type` parameter through `ScoringEngine` and `DataFetcher` calls.
- Updated `MarketDataHandler` to include `asset_type` in the cache key for market data.

### Stock Data Fetching
- Added `fetch_ticker_info` and `_get_info_cache_filepath` to `MarketDataHandler` in `src/optitrade/utils/data_fetcher.py` to fetch general ticker information (e.g., shares outstanding, market cap).
- Added `get_ticker_info` to `DataFetcher` in `src/optitrade/utils/data_fetcher.py`.
- Added `fetch_risk_free_rate` and `_get_risk_free_rate_cache_filepath` to `MarketDataHandler` in `src/optitrade/utils/data_fetcher.py` to fetch the 10-year US Treasury yield (`^TNX`).
- Added `get_risk_free_rate` to `DataFetcher` in `src/optitrade/utils/data_fetcher.py`.
- Ensured `fetch_financial_statement` in `MarketDataHandler` correctly handles `cashflow` data.

### New Model: FinancialRatioModel
- Created `src/optitrade/models/financial_ratio_model.py` for stock fundamental analysis.
- Implemented calculation and scoring for Price-to-Earnings (P/E) and Price-to-Book (P/B) ratios.
- Extended the model to include Debt-to-Equity (D/E), Earnings Per Share (EPS) Growth, Price-to-Sales (P/S), and EBITDA Margin ratios with corresponding scoring logic.
- Added `FinancialRatioModel` to `MODEL_WEIGHTS_DEFAULT`, `MODEL_WEIGHTS_STRONG_TREND`, and `MODEL_WEIGHTS_RANGING` in `src/optitrade/config.py`.

### Model Weight Optimization for Stocks
- Added new stock-specific model weight profiles (`MODEL_WEIGHTS_STOCK_DEFAULT`, `MODEL_WEIGHTS_STOCK_STRONG_TREND`, `MODEL_WEIGHTS_STOCK_RANGING`) to `src/optitrade/config.py`.
- Updated `ScoringEngine` in `src/optitrade/scoring/scoring_engine.py` to dynamically select model weight profiles based on the `asset_type` (crypto or stock) and market regime.

### New Model: DCFModel
- Created `src/optitrade/models/dcf_model.py` for Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) valuation.
- Implemented fetching of cash flow statements, ticker info, and risk-free rate within the model.
- Implemented calculation of Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC).
- Implemented historical Free Cash Flow (FCF) calculation and future FCF projection.
- Implemented Terminal Value calculation.
- Implemented intrinsic value per share calculation.
- Implemented scoring based on the upside potential derived from the intrinsic value and current market price.
- Fixed a bug where `risk_free_rate` was not correctly passed to the `_calculate_intrinsic_value` method.
